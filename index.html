Laptop Charger Compatibility Guide

Finding the right laptop charger requires more than matching the brand. Before buying a replacement adapter, check its electrical specifications and connector type.

1. Check Voltage

For barrel-type chargers, check that the adapter's output voltage meets the laptop manufacturer's requirements. USB-C Power Delivery uses negotiated voltage profiles instead.

2. Check Wattage

Choose an adapter with sufficient power capacity. A higher-wattage charger can be suitable when its other specifications and charging protocol are compatible.

3. Check Connector Type

Verify connector size, polarity, and any identification-pin requirements. A plug fitting into the socket does not guarantee compatibility.

4. Check USB-C Charging

Confirm that your laptop supports USB-C Power Delivery charging and that the adapter and cable support its required power profile.
